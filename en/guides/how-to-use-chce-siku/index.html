App guide

How to use Chcę Siku! to find, add and review a toilet

Chcę Siku! editorial teamPublished: 27 October 20264 min read
At a glance

Open Chcę Siku!, choose the facilities you need and select a suitable nearby toilet. Tap Prowadź for map and compass guidance. After your visit, leave a review or add a missing place. The app currently uses Polish labels; this guide explains the buttons in English so you can recognise them on your phone.

1. Open the app and find nearby toilets

Allow Chcę Siku! to use your phone's location. The Najbliżej Ciebie section means Nearest to you and lists places by distance. You can browse the map and place information without an account. If the position is missing or inaccurate, check the app's location permission and your phone's location settings.

Each place card can show distance, a cleanliness rating and facilities recorded for that location. Tap Mapa to see the map, or open a place to read its description, comments and available photos. Check the entrance as well as the pin: a toilet on the other side of a railway line may take longer to reach than its distance suggests.

2. Choose the facilities you need

The filters use these Polish labels:

  • Bezpłatna: free to use.
  • Woda: water available, useful when checking handwashing facilities. This is not a drinking-water certification.
  • Przewijak: baby changing table.
  • Dla osób z niepełnosprawnościami: marked as accessible for disabled people.
  • Parking: parking recorded at the location.
  • Prysznic: shower recorded at the location.

When you select several filters, results must match all of them. If the closest result is far away, try removing a non-essential filter. An unmarked facility may simply be missing from the record. Keep any feature essential to your access requirements and confirm details with the venue where necessary.

Check opening hours and any possible-closure warning. Unknown hours do not mean 24-hour access. The app's indication that a place exists is separate from its cleanliness rating.

3. Use Prowadź to orient yourself

In the place details, Prowadź opens guidance inside Chcę Siku! You can use a map view or an arrow showing the direction of your destination. The distance and estimated time help you orient yourself, but the arrow does not check every crossing, locked gate or other obstacle. Follow safe, permitted paths and look for the actual entrance.

For a walking route along streets, select Otwórz w mapach, meaning Open in maps. You can pass the destination to Google Maps, or to Apple Maps on supported devices. The in-app direction arrow should not be mistaken for turn-by-turn street navigation.

4. Leave a review after your visit

Sign in, open the place and choose Po wizycie - oceń miejscówkę, the review option after a visit. The guidance map also offers Oceń toaletę, meaning Rate the toilet. Answer Czy to miejsce istnieje? (Does this place exist?) and rate its cleanliness.

Choose Chcę podać szczegóły (I want to provide details) to contribute information about facilities, whether it is free, its name or opening hours. Only mark TAK (Yes) or NIE (No) for facts you checked. Then tap Zapisz ocenę (Save review).

If the toilet genuinely no longer exists, the form offers Zgłoś brak miejsca to report it missing. A toilet that is closed at night still exists: update its hours or leave a clear comment instead.

5. Add a toilet that is missing from the map

First check nearby pins to avoid adding the same toilet twice. Sign in and tap Dodaj (Add) at the bottom of the screen. In the Dodaj miejsce form:

  1. Enter a place name, choose its type and describe how to find the entrance.
  2. Record cleanliness and the facilities you have confirmed. Add whether it is free if you know.
  3. Enter opening hours, or choose Nie znam godzin otwarcia (I do not know the opening hours).
  4. Move the map so the pin marks the toilet's actual position.
  5. Add a photo if you can do so without photographing other users or private information.
  6. Confirm that the place is safe and legally accessible, then tap Zapisz (Save).

Do not add private property as a public facility or assume that permission to enter is automatic. If the app says a submission is waiting to be sent, check its status when you have a connection again.

Make your contribution useful to the next person

A short, specific note is often enough: Entrance beside the car park, Ask staff for the key or Changing table in a separate family room. Describe what you observed and, where helpful, when you visited. Do not guess about facilities you did not inspect.

Download Chcę Siku! for iPhone or Android and try the steps in a familiar area before a trip. For planning a visit somewhere new, read our guide to finding toilets while travelling. The button names above were checked against the app source in October 2026.
