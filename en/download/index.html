Before you head out.

How do I find a public toilet near me?

Open Gotta Pee!, allow location access and look at the nearby list or map. Choose the facilities you need, check the place details and use the directions feature. In US English, you may call the same place a public restroom or bathroom.

Is Gotta Pee! free?

The app is free to download on iPhone and Android. Ads may appear in the app. A free download does not mean that every toilet on the map is free to use.

Does it work outside Poland?

Yes. The map includes locations around the world. The database had 496,888 places on 20 October 2026; coverage and detail vary by region. Read about coverage and data sources.

Do I need an account?

You can browse places as a guest. Sign in to add places, upload photos or leave a review.

Is the app available in English?

Yes. Gotta Pee! is available in English on iPhone and Android. Download it from the App Store or Google Play, then follow our step-by-step guide to find, add and review toilets.

Are mapped toilets always open and accessible?

No. Check opening hours, reviews and access conditions. An accessibility label does not confirm every feature you might need, and missing opening hours do not mean 24-hour access.

Can I find a baby-changing table?

Use the Baby changing filter, then check the details and entrance. For an adult changing bench or hoist, check those specific facilities separately. Read the family guide.
