Control over your data

Delete your account and data

You can start permanent account deletion directly in the app or send us a request.

Quick linksIn the appBy emailWhat we deleteHow long it takes

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Chcę Siku! and sign in.
  2. Open the account menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Select “Usuń konto” (Delete account).
  4. Read the information and confirm your decision.
This action is permanent. Once the process is complete, the account and its associated data cannot be recovered.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, send an email from the address associated with your account. Use the subject “Usunięcie konta Chcę Siku!” (Chcę Siku! account deletion).

Send a request

Write an email

Address: chcesiku.cedrotomasz@gmail.com

For security, we may ask you to confirm that you own the account. Do not send your password or a photo of an identity document unless you receive specific, justified instructions.

What will be deleted

  • the account and sign-in data;
  • the email address, display name and account identifiers;
  • photos and content associated exclusively with the account, unless retention is required by law;
  • other personal data associated with the account.

Information about places useful to the public may be retained only after it has been permanently detached from the account and anonymised. Data needed to fulfil a legal obligation or protect against abuse may be retained for the necessary period.

Processing time

We fulfil requests without undue delay, usually within a few days and no later than the deadline required by applicable law. You will receive confirmation by email when the process is complete.

You can also request deletion of particular content without deleting the whole account. Describe the content clearly in your message.
